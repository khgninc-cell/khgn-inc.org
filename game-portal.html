The Journey

From the Trailhead to the Mountain Top: how do I get there from here?

Like the families who set out from Virginia for Kentucky, every KHGN volunteer begins with a long journey. You'll join the caravan, choose your own route, learn the dangers as you go, and finally climb to stake your claim. Choose your journey wisely. Volunteer roles only: the journey leads to greater responsibility within KHGN's volunteer network, never paid work.

  1. 🧭

    The Trailhead Open to everyone, 20 and older

    The beginning of the journey. Free interactive training and stories from Kentucky's past, open to the public.

  2. 🐂

    Joining the Caravan The Tipple Trial

    Every journey west starts by joining the caravan. The Tipple Trial teaches the essentials every traveler needs before setting out: privacy, copyright, and the records you'll handle along the way.

    Join the Caravan →
  3. 🛤️

    Trail Routes For enrolled volunteers

    Here the wagons separate, each following its own route toward its claim. Choose your journey wisely, and learn the dangers as you go. Each route is KHGN's subcommittee-based volunteer training, climbed one level at a time, with a banner at every step:

    • 🧭 Moderating on Facebook
    • 🥾 Workforce Development
    • 📣 Community Advisory
    • 🌄 More routes on the way
    How to Enroll →
  4. ⛰️

    The Base of the Mountain Your route complete

    Finish your Trail Route and you've reached the foot of the mountain, with the experience and trust earned along the way.

  5. 🌽

    The Mountain Top Committee and Director training

    Now the climb to claim your grant. Kentucky's early settlers proved their claims by raising a crop of corn and building a cabin; here, you plant your "corn" and build your "cabin" by completing KHGN's top-level training for committee and Director service.

    Coming Soon
  6. 📜

    Claiming the Grant Nomination and election

    With the Mountain Top behind you, you may put forward your nomination for a Director seat. If the Board elects you, your grant is accepted: you've met all the terms to claim it.

Please note: Training for committee, Director, or Officer service does not guarantee any position. These are not appointed positions. They are filled by vote of KHGN's Board of Directors under its Bylaws, and completing training, or being nominated, does not imply placement, immediate or otherwise.

Earn a banner at every step

Finish a training level and download your banner right away. From the leadership levels up, your role banner follows hands-on time and your supervisor's sign-off. Banners carry no personal information, so you can share them proudly.

How enrollment worksComplete the Tipple Trial, then send a message to KHGN's Facebook Page. We'll schedule a short phone call to meet you and go over the role. Once approved, you'll receive access to Trail Routes by email.
One step at a timeEveryone starts at the first level of their route. Each step up comes with training, hands-on time, and a supervisor's sign-off, and time requirements can be adjusted when roles need filling.
Climb at your own paceKHGN's teams share the load, so you can step back when life calls and pick up where you left off.

About the Trailhead

The KHGN Trailhead offers interactive ways to learn history and genealogy research skills, free and open to the public ages 20 and older. This is KHGN's unaccredited curriculum — a separate, formally accredited program is planned as a future addition. New content is sorted into the categories above based on what it teaches: core skills, research methods, historical stories, or serious history that stands on its own. Volunteer leadership training continues beyond the Trailhead, on the Trail Routes and the Mountain Top. Training for committee, Director, or Officer service does not guarantee any position; those seats are filled by vote of the Board. New additions are made regularly, so check back often.
