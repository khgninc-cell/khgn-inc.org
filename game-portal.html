About the Trailhead

The KHGN Trailhead offers interactive ways to learn history and genealogy research skills, free and open to the public ages 20 and older. This is KHGN's unaccredited curriculum — a separate, formally accredited program is planned as a future addition. New content is sorted into the categories above based on what it teaches: core skills, volunteer leadership, research methods, historical stories, or serious history that stands on its own. New additions are made regularly, so check back often.
