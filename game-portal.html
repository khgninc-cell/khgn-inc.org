About the Game Portal

The KHGN Inc. Game Portal is part of our Training Arcade, offering interactive ways to learn history and genealogy research skills. New content is sorted into the categories above based on what it teaches: required volunteer skills, general historical stories, practical research methods, or serious history that deserves a dignified telling rather than a game score. New additions are made regularly, so check back often.
