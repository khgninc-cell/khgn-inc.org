Why Green Pasture is free: Genealogy education is too often paid, fragmented, or locked behind course fees. KHGN believes free, well-built, honest genealogy education — both how to research responsibly and the real texture of Kentucky's history, including its hardest parts — is worth building for everyone, not selling to a few.
Kentucky History & Genealogy Network, Inc. — Green Pasture is a free public resource. No account or login required.
