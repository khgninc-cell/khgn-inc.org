History | London's Sentinel-Echo traces its roots to the Mountain Echo, first published in Barbourville in 1873 and moved to London in 1875. In 1908, the owner of the London Sentinel bought the Echo, and the two papers later combined under the Sentinel-Echo name, used since 1919. Barbourville's Mountain Advocate, founded in 1904, shares the other half of the old name: two papers whose names, together, echo the original.
Source | The Sentinel-Echo, "About Us"; Laurel County History Museum timeline; Kentucky Genealogical Society
