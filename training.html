Volunteer Training

Training for KHGN Volunteers

KHGN’s volunteers train step by step on Trail Routes, climbing one level at a time with a banner at every step. The core training is free and open to everyone; the Trail Routes are for enrolled volunteers.

Already Enrolled?

Go to Training Home, KHGN’s private training area for enrolled volunteers.

Go to Training Home

You’ll be asked to sign in with the email address KHGN approved for you. A one-time code will be sent to that address. Your training progress is saved on the device you use.

Not Enrolled Yet?

Every volunteer begins with the Tipple Trial, KHGN’s free core training, then sends us a message to talk about the role that fits them.

The Trail Routes

Each route belongs to one of KHGN’s subcommittees. Everyone starts at the first level, and each step up comes with training, hands-on time, and a supervisor’s sign-off.
