About Kentucky History and Genealogy Network Inc.

Kentucky History and Genealogy Network Inc. (KHGN Inc.) is an independent, sovereign educational non-profit organization serving family history researchers and genealogists across the Commonwealth of Kentucky. Our Facebook social media hub connects members with statewide hubs and multi-county district communities covering topics such as coal mining heritage, cemetery documentation, and local folklore. We rely on dedicated community members, so if you're interested in contributing your time, please visit our volunteer and board recruitment page to learn more about open roles. As a non-profit organization, KHGN Inc. depends on public support to sustain its mission — you can make a meaningful impact through our secure donation portal. Finally, researchers and volunteers looking to build new skills can visit our educational training arcade for regional field presentation and archival skill development, or explore our full game portal for more interactive learning tools.
