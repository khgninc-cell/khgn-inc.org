Volunteer and Board Recruitment

Volunteer with KHGN, Inc.

Help Kentuckians discover their family history. Every volunteer role begins with free training, and every route grows at your own pace.

How to Become a Volunteer

  1. Complete the Tipple TrialOur free core training covers privacy, copyright, record release dates, and conflicts of interest. Take it at your own pace, in as many sittings as you like.
  2. Send us a messageMessage KHGN’s Facebook Page letting us know you’ve completed the Tipple Trial, and which kind of role interests you.
  3. Talk with usWe’ll schedule a short phone call to meet you and go over the role that fits you best.
  4. Get approved and start your routeOnce approved, you’ll receive access to your Trail Route training by email.

Volunteer Trail Routes

Each route is a series of steps you climb as your experience grows. Start at the first step, and move up when you’re ready.

Moderating on Facebook

Help keep KHGN’s county and statewide groups welcoming, accurate, and on topic.

Route: Contributing Steward → Moderator → County Administrator → District Administrator → Regional Administrator

District Administrators support a small group of County Administrators, carrying their questions and reports up to the Board and committees, and bringing guidance back down, so no one carries too much alone.

Workforce Development

Welcome new volunteers, guide them through training, and help them find the right route.

Route: Trail Greeter → Trail Guide → Pathfinder → Trailblazer → Trailkeeper

Community Advisory

Be the public’s voice inside KHGN, sharing what’s working and what could be better.

Route: Regional Advisor

Serve on the Board of Directors

KHGN, Inc. has open seats on its Board of Directors. Directors help guide the organization’s work through committees such as Communication, Secretary, Finance, and Grant and Fundraising. Directors serve two-year terms, with elections held every year for half of the seats, so the Board grows steadily.

If you’d like to learn about serving as a Director, send us a message and we’ll be glad to talk with you.
