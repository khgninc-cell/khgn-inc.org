Proving Your Claim

Kentucky's early land claims were famously tangled. Surveys overlapped, and many settlers had to prove their claims in court, weighing deeds, testimony, and records to decide who was right. Genealogy works the same way. Learn to weigh evidence like a court, test it against the record, and reach a verdict that's never fully certain.
