Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2, 2026

In short: we collect only what you type into the volunteer form and the email address you use to sign in to volunteer training. We use it only for volunteering, we never sell it, and we'll delete it whenever you ask. Our training pages, County Hubs, and other website pages do not send your answers, progress, or reading to us.

Who we are

This notice is provided by Kentucky History and Genealogy Network, Inc. (KHGN, Inc., "KHGN," "we," or "us"), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to preserving and sharing Kentucky's history and genealogy. It covers KHGN's website at khgn-inc.org, including the County Hubs, the volunteer interest form, the Trailhead training pages and the Tipple Trial, and the sign-in for KHGN's private volunteer training site.

What we collect

When you submit the volunteer interest form, we receive the information you choose to provide:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to include it
  • Your Kentucky county of interest
  • The volunteer areas you select
  • Anything you write in the message box

Please don't include sensitive information in your message, such as Social Security numbers, financial details, or private information about other people.

Most of KHGN's website, including the home page, the County Hubs and county pages, the Get Involved page, and the research guides, does not ask for or collect any personal information.

Volunteer training sign-in

KHGN's private training site for enrolled volunteers is protected by a sign-in. To sign in, you enter the email address KHGN approved for you, and a one-time code is sent to that address. The sign-in service records that your approved email address signed in, so we can keep the training site limited to enrolled volunteers. We don't use that information for anything else.

What the training pages store

The Tipple Trial and other training levels may save your progress, such as whether you've finished a level or its reading, in your own web browser on your own device. That information stays on your device and is not sent to KHGN. You can clear it at any time using the reset link on each level or your browser's settings. Your quiz answers are not recorded or sent to us. The same is true of self-check tools such as Rate Your Research and Build Your Case: what you type there stays in your browser.

The Trailhead age check

Before opening the Trailhead and its training pages, visitors are asked for their date of birth to confirm they are 20 or older. The date is checked in your own browser and immediately discarded. It is never sent to KHGN or stored. Your browser keeps only a simple "passed" marker until you close the tab, and, if the age check isn't passed, a note of when you may try again.

How we use your information

We use the information from the volunteer form and the training sign-in only to:

  • Contact you about volunteering with KHGN
  • Match you with volunteer roles and the county or region you're interested in
  • Keep a record of volunteer interest so we can follow up with you
  • Give enrolled volunteers access to the private training site

We will not add you to marketing lists or use your information for any unrelated purpose without asking you first.

Who can see it

Your submission is seen only by the KHGN administrators responsible for volunteer coordination. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We share it outside KHGN only:

  • With the services that deliver and store our email, form submissions, and training sign-ins, which process it on our behalf
  • When required by law

Services that help run these pages

KHGN's website relies on a few outside services, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you visit:

  • Web hosting: KHGN's pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard visitor logs.
  • Security and delivery: Cloudflare delivers KHGN's pages, protects the site from automated attacks and bots, and provides the sign-in for the volunteer training site.
  • Google Fonts: the lettering on these pages loads from Google.
  • Email and form delivery: form submissions reach us through our email provider and, if enabled, a form-processing service.
  • Links to Facebook: KHGN's county and statewide groups are on Facebook. When you follow a link to a Facebook group, Facebook's own privacy policy applies there.

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep volunteer form information only as long as it's useful for volunteer coordination. We review our records periodically and delete information we no longer need. When a volunteer leaves KHGN, their training sign-in access is removed. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

How we protect it

We limit access to volunteer information to the administrators who need it, and we store it in password-protected accounts. No method of storing or sending information online is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share with us.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we have about you
  • Correct information that's wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop contacting you

To make a request, email privacy@khgn-inc.org.

Age requirement

The volunteer interest form is intended for adults 20 years of age and older. If you are under 20, please do not submit the form. If we learn that we've received information from someone under 20, we will delete it.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about this notice or your information can be sent to privacy@khgn-inc.org.
